## Cause found, measured not guessed
dance-recreation-20261031 built 217 frames @48fps by 50% alpha
cross-blends between consecutive poses (build_recreation.py:
Image.blend(a, b, 0.5)). Every odd frame therefore shows TWO
forearms at once — the white near forearm at pose A and the dark
far forearm at pose B, both semi-transparent — alternating with
clean key frames at 48fps. That is the black/white forearm flash.
Whole-frame mean luminance MISSES it (forearm is small vs frame;
original whole-frame general flashes = 0). The forearm-box
pixel-reversal metric below is what catches it.

Forearm boxes (1920x1080 dance-test layout, scaled to analysis
width): L x390-440, R x525-570, y190-280 at 960x540.
Original, frame triples with >5% of forearm-box pixels reversing
luminance by >30: L 29/215, R 30/215, max 23.6% (L, frame 41),
22.9% (R, frame 41).

## Industry standard wired, not just listed
- WCAG 2.3.1 Three Flashes or Below Threshold (W3C): no content
  flashes more than 3 times in any 1-second period, or below the
  general/red thresholds. General flash = pair of opposing
  luminance changes >=10% of maximum, darker <0.80.
- Ofcom/Harding, via Epilepsy Foundation consensus (Harding et
  al., Epilepsia 2005): potentially harmful flash = opposing
  luminance changes >=20 cd/m2, at >=3 Hz, occupying >=0.006
  steradians (~25% of a 10-degree field); any transition to/from
  saturated red is harmful irrespective of luminance.
- Animation practice applied: NO cross-blends, keys only, motion
  on 2s (12 drawings/sec, normal movement), one consistent
  shading per arm per key, holds instead of blends, fast swings
  held to 2s in v2 (see below).
- GATE IS A PROXY, NOT A CERTIFICATION: flash_check.py is a
  WCAG-inspired proxy on 0-255 luminance, NOT cd/m2, NOT the
  paid Harding FPA, NOT PEAT (PEAT's licence prohibits commercial
  TV/film/game use). It must never be presented as Harding/PEAT
  certified. It is wired as a mandatory pre-delivery gate in
  this folder: exit 0 PASS, exit 1 FAIL.
