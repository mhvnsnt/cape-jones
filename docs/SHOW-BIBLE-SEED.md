# CAPE JONES — show bible seed

- Lead: CAPE JONES (formerly Space Ghost — proprietary rename, owner 2026-10-14).
- Tone/direction (owner, in his own words, 2026-10-29): Comedic, deadpan, accidentally funny. Wild card and unpredictable, mixed with the mundane and the deadpan. The setting does the work. The comedy lives in the actions, and just as much in the lack of action. The dialogue is straightforward and lands plain. It lives in the show's writing, gags and canon, and across the whole network umbrella of the owner's shows, all of it in Space Ghost tone and language. It is the tone and style of the owner's own shows and writing, mixed with Space Ghost Coast to Coast and Home Movies.
- Owner example (2026-10-29): a montage of Cape Jones outside doing the weird Charlie Brown dance in one spot, facing one way then the other, the same simple loop repeating, while the lighting cycles from normal daytime to various LED colors to natural day and night and back, and the environment, background and foreground change around him. Tamagotchi style digital pet music plays. Presentation mixes the Charlie Brown dance animations with Space Ghost and Gumball, like the SpongeBob dancing at his house scene. Simple repetitive animation: the world changes, the dance does not.
