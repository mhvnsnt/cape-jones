## How to use this sheet (editor notes)

- Scene 6 runs from ~3:20 to the end of a 6+ minute episode, so the
  fight is roughly 2.5–3 minutes. The pieces below are 2:25–8:11 each —
  **they play as excerpts trimmed to beat length at sound lock**,
  crossfaded at beat boundaries. No piece needs to play in full.
- Order follows the score's own dramatic arc (arrival → menace →
  dread → climax → dawn), which mirrors the fight's arc. That is why
  the pieces below are NOT in film order throughout — they are in
  fight order, and the film-order arc is preserved in spirit:
  tension builds, the predator peaks, the sun rises.
- Piece names below use the PR #250 scheme (transfer + lineage +
  film section + timestamp). Full scheme name per piece: "Nosferatu
  (1922) — VHS synth score (Nosferatu1922VHS transfer, MoMA 1947
  print) — [section] — [mm:ss–mm:ss]".
- If any piece lands wrong to the owner's ear, it moves — owner
  ear-check rules. Alternates are listed per beat.

## The cue: Scene 6 beat by beat

### Beat 1 — Arrival (portal_03_arrival, Cape lands, gets bearings)
**Piece: P05 — The coach ride, negative footage (19:18–21:43, 2:25)**
Why: this piece opens right at the score's quiet break — the music
drops to near-silence at 19:20, then rebuilds into eerie negative-
footage strangeness. That is exactly landing in a world that is wrong:
quiet, then wrong. It says "you are not home anymore" without a single
loud hit, so Cape's bearings beat plays under it, not over it.
Alternate: P04 — The mountain road (15:55–19:18), if arrival wants
more forward motion and less eeriness.

### Beat 2 — Sizing up (predator waits, "I don't even want to be here.")
**Piece: P07 — At the castle (25:55–28:54, 2:59)**
Why: tense, held-back, two figures in a seat of power looking at each
other. It holds still the way Pantera holds still — predatory
patience, not action yet. It leaves room for Cape's line and Pantera's
growl greeting (pulled audio, sound lock) to sit on top.
Alternate: P02 — "You can't escape destiny" (3:31–9:09), if the
staredown wants the warning-intertitle weight instead.

### Beat 3 — Opening exchanges (hand cannons vs clinch, hold, throw)
**Piece: P03 — Hutter and Nina at home (9:09–15:55, 6:46, excerpt)**
Why: this is the journey piece — it contains the 13:38 passage the
owner already ear-checked and liked. Forward motion, back-and-forth
energy, the longest driving passage in the set. It carries exchanges
without peaking early. Play from the piece start; trim to beat length.
Alternate: P12 — Aboard the ship, the hold (51:22–58:49, excerpt),
if exchanges want something more relentless and less melodic.

### Beat 4 — Pantera hurt (down to one hit from death)
**Piece: P17 — Nina's vigil (73:43–81:46, 8:03, excerpt)**
Why: a vigil is holding your breath while death stands next to
someone. That is the one-hit-left moment — the whole fight narrows
to one hit, and the music narrows with it: tense, waiting, dread, no
triumph in it. It makes the comeback that follows feel earned instead
of random.
Alternate: P14 — The plague dead (64:15–67:32), if the hurt beat
wants grim and blunt instead of held breath.

### Beat 5 — Comeback chain grapple (KESSHOU chain finishes Cape)
**Piece: P09 — Nosferatu's shadow over Hutter (32:27–40:38, 8:11, excerpt)**
Why: this is the score's menace peak — the iconic shadow passage,
the predator unleashed. The chain grapple is Pantera at his most
Pantera, and this is the music at its most Pantera. It is the fight's
climax and it should sound like it. Cut in hard at the grapple's
first hold; let it carry through the chain.
Alternate: P16 — Hutter returned (70:13–73:43), if the chain wants
urgency and warning rather than pure menace.

### Beat 6 — Finish + freeze/tag
**Piece: P18 — Finale, dawn at the window (81:46–84:27, 2:41)**
Why: the sun rises, the master is dead, the final intertitle, "The
End." The fight ends the way the film ends: light after the
nightmare. It resolves the whole cue instead of just stopping it.
After the freeze, script tag rule applies: M5 reprise of M1 (Ghost
Surf Rock) or silence — owner's call at approval, not this sheet's.
Alternate: none — the finale is the finale. If the freeze wants
silence instead, cut P18 at the final hit and hold silence.
