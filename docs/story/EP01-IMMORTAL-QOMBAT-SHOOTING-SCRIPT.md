## 0. THE RULES THIS SCRIPT OBEYS (read first)

1. **The owner's brief is the foundation.** Every beat below traces to the
   brief or to a cited canon doc. Anything not in the brief or canon is
   marked TBD in §8 — never filled in silently.
2. **In-universe name: IMMORTAL QOMBAT. NEVER "Western Heart" in the show.**
   "Western Heart" is the game repo's name, not the show's. No character,
   title card, or narration in this episode says "Western Heart."
3. **Cape Jones's voice = the owner, always.** Every Cape line is marked
   `[USER VO — to be recorded]`. His voice is NEVER synthesized.
4. **Fighter dialogue = pulled game audio ONLY.** Pantera has NO invented
   lines in this script. His dialogue slots cite the audio source and are
   filled at sound lock (§6).
5. **Music = no-copyright ONLY.** Every cue logs artist / track / license
   (§7). No licensed, no Content ID-encumbered, no "probably fine" tracks.
6. **All characters are adults.** No minors, no exceptions.
7. **Tekken names stay OUT of in-universe writing.** Technical references
   (rig sources, moveset sources, audio sources) appear ONLY in production
   notes, never in dialogue, narration, or on-screen in-universe text.
8. **Fighters do NOT come out of the TV.** Cape flies INTO the TV. This was
   explicitly corrected by the owner and is locked.
9. **Arenas are NOT Tekken 3 arenas.** The owner: T3 arenas are "scrambled."

10. **Casting runs in rig-quality order, best rigs first** (standing

## 2. STORY IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Cape Jones, a hero who doesn't want to be one, is killing time at home in
his recliner under violet LED light. He turns on the TV, flips channels,
and lands on a fighting game called IMMORTAL QOMBAT. He plays. He sees a
fighter on screen. He drops the controller, says he's going to go fight
that guy — and then he does, literally: he flies into the TV and lands in
the game's arena, face to face with PANTERA, a panther wrestler who fights
because fighting is what he is. The joke is the parody of entering Mortal
Kombat — except for Cape, it's not a game anymore, and he never asked to
be here.
