# BROTHERMAN — locked likeness + continuity rules

**Owner direction: 2026-11-23 07:02–07:15 EST. Locked 2026-11-23 07:15 EST.**
**Name: BROTHERMAN — all caps, ALL ONE WORD.** Not "Brother Man", not "Brother".
He calls everyone "brother", everyone calls him "brother".
He appears in the **second full episode**. The first full episode is not made yet —
in it, Cape Jones goes into the game, into Immortal Kombat, a mix of 2D and 3D.
BROTHERMAN is episode 2, not episode 1.
He says a line like "keep up with the Joneses" to Cape Jones.

## Locked likeness — always, every time, unless a named exception below

Use the reference images in `production/2d-parking-lot/brotherman/`. Do not invent
a new look. Do not drift him scene to scene.

- **Body / face (PERFECT — locked, owner 2026-11-23 07:11 EST):** big muscular
  wrestler, strong jaw, thick blond horseshoe mustache, tanned skin, cartoon
  comic-book style, bold black outlines, flat bright colors. Reference:
  `brotherman-v4-cape-jones-correct.webp` — seafoam-blue shirt, pink
  BROTHERMAN text, Hogan face, correct Cape Jones beside him. That physique
  and face is the likeness to save and reuse. (The v2 concept file with the
  same physique is NOT in the repo: it had the incorrect Cape Jones and the
  owner said to delete it, not save it, 2026-11-23 07:21 EST.)
- **Outfit — Color 1 = bright seafoam blue / cyan, Color 2 = pink, a little
  purple.** Blue tank top, BROTHERMAN in big pink Hulkamania-style font.
  Long tie-dye tights, pink + blue with a little purple, like Hulk Hogan's
  tie-dye long tights. White wristbands (probably white, owner call). Blue
  boots matching Color 1, pink laces. Black wrestling belt.
  Hogan's rule, researched 2026-11-23: a 2-color system — bandana one color,
  shirt the other, tights tie-dye of both colors, wristbands and boots match
  one of the two colors; the swap (Hollywood: red shirt + tie-dye tights +
  yellow bandana) is how Color 1 and Color 2 swap across attires.
- **Hair, default / no bandana: bald top, long blond hair at the sides and
  back only.** Bald on top only, long hair left at sides/back, nothing else
  changed. Reference: `brotherman-v3-exact-bald-top.webp` (exact edit of the
  full-hair image, just the bald top).
- **Bandana: sometimes blue, sometimes pink. Original first appearance is
  PINK bandana.** Most of the time he does NOT wear a bandana. Reference
  (pink bandana): `brotherman-v3-pink-bandana.webp`.
- **REJECTED, never again:** the v1 sheet — purple cowl / white suit like Cape
  Jones (owner, 2026-11-23 07:07 EST). He is NOT dressed like Cape Jones, and
  the face did not look enough like Hulk Hogan.

## Continuity rules — no AI-slop inconsistency (owner, 2026-11-23 07:15 EST)

BROTHERMAN always appears like the locked likeness above, every time, unless
it is one of the named exceptions. No random scene-to-scene flips.

- **Exception 1 — old him / flashback ONLY:** full blond hair on top.
  Reference: `brotherman-v3-no-bandana-full-hair.webp`.
  The joke (owner, 2026-11-23 07:13 EST): he shows a picture of full-hair
  him and says "this used to be me" — he puts the picture up, then pulls it
  down and it's just him bald. Barely anything's different, he's just bald
  on top now. The pair for the gag is the full-hair image vs the exact
  bald-top edit of the same image.
- **Exception 2 — bandana on / off ONLY:** when he visibly takes it off or
  puts it on, OR as a deliberate joke where people look away and he takes it
  off / puts it on and Cape Jones / people notice when they look back.
  **Never** one scene with the bandana, the next without, the next with it
  again, with no visible change and no joke. Any bandana change must be
  on-purpose, visible, or a noticed look-away gag.
- **Pre-delivery check for any BROTHERMAN shot:** likeness matches the locked
  references? Hair state is default bald-top, bandana (pink/blue/none) is the
  one the scene calls for, and any change from the previous shot is visible
  or a noticed gag? If not, the shot does not ship.

## Cape Jones likeness rule (owner, 2026-11-23 07:12 EST) — also locked

**NEVER make Cape Jones in a way that's away from his likeness.**
Correct likeness, from `rig/assembly-preview-v8.png` and the boards: purple
cowl covering the top of his head, white eyes, tan skin on the lower face,
stern mouth, purple collar/neck, white skin-tight muscular suit with a small
purple V emblem on the chest, white belt, long black cape to his ankles,
white gloves/boots.
**NO** suit jacket, **NO** lapels, **NO** black gloves, **NO** black boots,
**NO** purple bald head, **NO** black eye-mask alone.
Generated v2/v4 BROTHERMAN images got this wrong (jacket/lapels/black
gloves); the corrected version is
`brotherman-v4-cape-jones-correct.webp`.
