# Cape Jones House Design

## The Vibe

Normal-looking suburban house. Aqua Teen Hunger Force energy — the kind of rental house that looks completely unremarkable from the street. Nothing about the exterior says "superhero lives here."

That's the joke. The most boring house on the block has a ghost cave in the basement.

## Exterior

- **Style**: Single-story ranch, beige siding, brown shingle roof. Built in the 80s, hasn't been updated since.
- **Front yard**: Patchy grass, one sad bush, a mailbox that's slightly crooked.
- **Driveway**: Cracked concrete. No car (he flies everywhere, why would he need one).
- **Neighborhood**: Quiet suburb near the beach. You can smell salt in the air. Palm trees two streets over.
- **Distinguishing feature**: None. That's the point.

## Interior — Living Room

Where most "Chilling at Home" scenes happen.

- **Layout**: Couch facing a TV. Coffee table with the LED remote on it. Recliner in the corner (his spot for the cup-lift thinker moment).
- **Walls**: Off-white. One poster of Nosferatu (1922) — framed, slightly crooked.
- **LED strips**: Behind the TV, along the ceiling cove. This is where the LED controller presets play.
- **Coffee table**: Always has a mug on it (coffee or tea). The mug changes but there's always one.
- **The recliner**: Worn leather, his thinking chair. Cup-lift happens here.

## Interior — Kitchen

- **Style**: Dated but clean. White appliances, laminate counters.
- **LED strips**: Under-cabinet. Usually on a warm setting.
- **Cake Jones scenes happen here.** Apron hangs on a hook by the stove.
- **Fridge**: Covered in magnets. One photo of the beach. Nothing else personal.

## Interior — Bedroom

- **Style**: Minimal. Bed, dresser, headboard LED strip.
- **He rarely sleeps.** He's usually in the living room or the ghost cave.
- **Closet**: Full of identical white suits. One purple cowl on a shelf.

## Interior — Bathroom

- **No LEDs.** Harsh white overhead bulb. He hates it in here.
- **Running gag**: every bathroom scene is lit terribly on purpose.

## The Basement — Ghost Cave

Nobody knows about it. He rarely goes down there.

- **Entrance**: Behind a bookshelf in the living room. The bookshelf swings open (it squeaks, he keeps meaning to fix it).
- **Stairs**: Concrete, single flickering teal LED strip along the wall. Always on ghost-mode.
- **The cave**: Larger than the house footprint (don't think about it). Stone walls, old tech, a big monitor wall showing... static. Mostly static.
- **What he does down here**: Unknown. He goes down, stays for a while, comes back up. Never talks about it.
- **Lighting**: Always ghost-mode teal. He never changes it. This is the one LED preset that's locked.

## The "Chilling at Home" Scene Layout

From the owner's description (2026-10-15):
- **Scene 1**: Living room. Standing, snapping fingers, moving neck/head side to side, vibing to Nosferatu. LED: `purple-pulse`.
- **Scene 2**: Recliner. Leaned forward pensively, cup-lift (raise, slurp, lower, raise). LED: `chill-blue`.
- **Scene 3**: Boring/mundane to Nosferatu — dancing then falling, or reading a book. LED: whatever he left it on.
