# Kick elbow fix — forearm/fist stay connected at the elbow — 2026-11-01

Mars, 2026-11-01 18:35 CST, reacting to
`kick-fixed-left-vertical-NOCROP-20261101.mp4`:
"when he widens his arms you disconnected his forearm and fist and stuff

## Anatomy first

The elbow is a hinge joint. The upper arm ends at the elbow ball centre,
the forearm starts at that same elbow ball centre, and both rotate about
that shared pivot. They never separate, never float, never gap.
The wrist is never cut: the lower piece is forearm + gauntlet + fist whole,
the hand is the child of the forearm, the forearm is the child of the upper.

Rig ball centres used (v7c, cut-limbs-v7):
- Arm_Front (our left, LEG/ARM L): elbow ball (60, 222) in upper-piece
  coordinates, shoulder anchor (76, 30), so the ball-to-shoulder vector is
  (-16, 192).
- Arm_Back (our right, R): elbow ball (99, 222), shoulder anchor (76, 30),
  vector (23, 192).
- Lower pieces verified in this job: Arm_Front_Lower top row y=0 spans
  x 28-92, centre 60.0; Arm_Back_Lower top row y=0 spans x 67-131,
  centre 99.0. So the lower anchor (ball_x, 0) used by `bc.limb` is correct
  — the lower ball is at the top of the lower piece, not at its image centre.
  The disconnect was NOT lower-piece geometry.

## What was disconnected, and why

The NOCROP kick builder copied the arm code incorrectly. In
`kick-crop-fix-20261101/build_kick_crop_fix.py`, the elbow was computed as:

```
elbows[side] = (shoulder_x + rot_vec(vec, arm_upper)[0] * SCALE,
                shoulder_y + rot_vec(vec, arm_lower)[1] * SCALE)
```

x from the upper-arm angle, y from the LOWER-arm angle. That is two
different pivots, not one hinge. `bc.figure` in `build_chains.py` does it
correctly, with both components from `arm_upper`.

That is why the fault only appears when he widens his arms:
- Stand (upper 0, lower 0, both arms): old gap 0.0 rig px, both arms.
- Chamber (upper -25/+25, lower +30/-30): old gap 7.0 rig px (L),
  13.1 rig px (R).
- Extension (upper -55/+45, lower +30/-30): old gap **73.4 rig px (L),
  55.4 rig px (R)** — the forearm, cap and fist were pasted that far
  below where the bicep actually ends, so a visible gap opened at the
  elbow, worst exactly at the widened-arms extension pose Mars flagged.

No in-place PIL `rotate(..., expand=False)` on a piece alone was used to
hide this: `bc.limb` rotates a full-canvas layer about the joint, so
rotation itself does not clip. The gap was the elbow coordinate, not a
rotation clip.

## Fix

In `build_kick_elbow_fix.py`, the elbow is now one shared pivot, both
components from `arm_upper`, exactly as `bc.figure`:

```
elbows[side] = (shoulder_x + rot_vec(vec, arm_upper)[0] * SCALE,
                shoulder_y + rot_vec(vec, arm_upper)[1] * SCALE)
```

The upper limb is rotated about the shoulder, its ball lands on that
elbow; the lower limb is pasted at that same elbow with anchor
(ball_x, 0) and rotated about it; the cap is centred on that same elbow.
Upper end and forearm start are therefore the same point, by construction.
