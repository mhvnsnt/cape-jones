# 3. Acknowledgement vs Completion Language
- Acknowledgement words: "received", "noted", "started", "running",
  "waiting on X". These mean: I heard it / began it, nothing more.
- Completion words: "done", "completed", "landed", "verified".
  These mean: I personally finished it AND checked it this turn.
- Never use a completion word as an acknowledgement. If the only thing
  that happened in this turn was a reply, the reply says "started" or
  "received" — it does not say "done".
- Template correction: a Done/Next/Need reply may only put a step
  under "Done" if that step is COMPLETED + VERIFIED. Delegated or
  pending steps go under "Next" with their status (running/waiting).
