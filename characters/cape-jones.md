## The character

- **Name:** CAPE JONES
- **Archetype:** washed-up cosmic cape-hero, still performing like it's his premiere.
- **Body gag** (Johnny Bravo rule — one exaggeration, committed fully): THE CAPE. Enormous, dramatic, billows with no wind, gets caught in doors, upstages him constantly. The most competent character on the show.
- **Want:** to be watched being heroic.
- **Flaw:** performs heroism instead of doing it.
- **Voice:** big, theatrical, mid-century hero-announcer cadence — undercut constantly by the cape and the sidekick. (Voice casting: owner's call.)
- **Powers:** flight, power-band blasts (gold wrist bands), cosmic durability — all real, all deployed at the wrong moment for the wrong reason.
- **Catchphrase:** TBD by owner. (Bible premise #6 is the in-show origin of it.)

## Likeness anchors (must stay consistent — Johnny Bravo style-bible rule)

1. Black domino mask with glowing yellow eyes.
2. Cape volume: navy outside, crimson lining, always too big for the room.
3. Gold power bands on both wrists.
4. White segmented armored suit; white boots.

## What he is NOT

- Not a parody of any single copyrighted hero — the Space Ghost production materials are study reference; the character, name, and design are original to this show.
- Not Superman: Superman remains copyrighted until 2034; no S-shield, no Daily Planet, no kryptonite, none of it.
