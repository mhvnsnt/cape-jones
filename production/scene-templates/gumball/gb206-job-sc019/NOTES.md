# GB206 "The Job" — Sc019: amazed two-shot on a photographic background plate

**Cast (named, from the file):** Darwin, Gumball.
**Shape:** 56 frames @ 25 fps · stage 1920×1080 · source `GB206JOB_Sc019_V007_Clean_MB.fla`

## Construction
- The background is a real photograph of a house and picket fence (Gumball's mixed-media signature) — flat vector puppets composited over a photo plate on the `background` layer.
- Both puppets play a 'squee' reaction — hands pressed to cheeks, star-pupil eyes — stacked as separate scene layers (DARWIN over the Gumball puppet layer).
- The comedy timing is offset: Darwin holds the delight for the whole 56 frames while Gumball's face falls to a worried blank stare around frame 35. Same frame, two reaction clocks.
- Eye/mouth comps (EYESCOMP2/MOUTH2, 199–223 kf each) carry the scene; bodies are nearly still. In close-up, the face system IS the animation.
- Second-generation comps (the '2' versions) are used: the library keeps versioned face systems per production block.

## Techniques (evidence in scene-anatomy.json)

- face-comp-driven — bodies still, comps perform.

## What Cape Jones steals
Two things: (1) photo plates are fair game under flat puppets — shoot/find a plate, light the puppets flat, done; (2) offset reaction clocks — when two characters share a reaction shot, never sync their beats; the joke lives in the offset.
