# GB2215 "The Apology" — Sc142: Gumball falls apart and reassembles (body-part gag)

**Cast (named, from the file):** Gumball (solo — in several pieces).
**Shape:** 75 frames @ 25 fps · stage 1920×1080 · source `GB2215APOLOGY_Sc142_V005_Clean_JA.fla`

## Construction
- The joke is staged by breaking the puppet into separately placed pieces — no morphing. Frame 1 is only legs + tail walking in alone; the scene trusts the audience to read dislocated parts as the character.
- Head, torso and limbs are placed as independent scene elements, splayed in impossible positions (multiple legs, head too big for the body, parts trailing behind), and the pieces progressively recombine until a strained, teeth-gritted whole Gumball stands at frame 75.
- The head comp holds 805 keyframes — more than the body puppet (555): the giant gritted-teeth head is the visual anchor while the body is in pieces.
- Background is a locked school hallway (tile wall, red window frames): all motion is character-side, which makes the part chaos readable.
- A 'Roughaurel' layer (rough pass by animator Laurel) ships inside the clean file — rough and clean coexist in production files.

## What Cape Jones steals
