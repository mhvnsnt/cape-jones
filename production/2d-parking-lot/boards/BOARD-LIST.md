# CAPE JONES — 2D Parking-Lot LED Remote Test Short — BOARD LIST

**Track: 2D** (drawn show — separate from the 3D test-short lane; no 3D files touched)
**Title: PURPLE LOT** — two-word meme-bait title in the $UPEREGO style. It carries the whole
premise in two words: the lot, and the light. The joke is not the remote, it is that an empty
parking lot at night turns purple because one guy decided it should — the title is the
punchline image, quotable the same way $UPEREGO titles quote the episode's line.
**Runtime:** 480 frames @ 24fps = 20s. Drawn changes on twos (12 drawings/sec) — these boards
are the KEYS, not in-betweens, not holds, not stills with pans.
**Cast:** Jones + the Cape alone. The Fan kid does NOT appear in this short — the sincere beat
lands through the lighting shift itself ("the world gets better for one second"), not through
a second character; the Fan stays the audience surrogate for later shorts.
**Palette (locked, sheet 03):** suit pure white #FFFFFF (stays blown out, tint on rim/glow
only), cowl & logo deep violet #3E1473, skin #E5B28C, cape & shadows absolute black #000000,
2–4px rim light. White eyes default; cartoony pupils only sometimes for emphasis. The old
yellow-cape / red-bands colorway from design-sheet.png is NEVER used.
**Camera:** framings match the 3D test short's proven shots (cpu_f0001 wide, cpu_f0230 medium).

## Gaps — stated plainly

- **CJ-PL-01, CJ-PL-03, CJ-PL-07 were NOT drawn.** The media image-generation tool
  declined those three generation requests (its own safety check). Per standing instruction I
  did not retry, reword, or route around those three requests. The four drawn panels are
  verified on-model (read back with my own eyes — see verification notes). The three missing
  keys need a different drawing route (hand-drawn by the pose lane, a later re-attempt, or the
  owner's call) — they are NOT covered by reusing another panel.
- **VOICE GAP (CJ-PL-07):** "ah, that's better" is the owner's recorded voice only. Never
  synthesized, never voiced by any agent. Flagged in the table above.
- **Continuity note (not a redraw trigger):** the drawn panels show parked cars in the
  background (02, 04, 06); the 3D wide proof (cpu_f0001) shows an empty lot. If the lot must
  read empty, the backgrounds get cleaned in the animation pass — the character keys are
  unaffected.

## Verification (own-eyes read-back of every drawn panel)

- **CJ-PL-02 — PASS.** White suit, violet cowl, black cape, white eyes, orange sodium wash,
  hand drifting to the remote at the belt, cape stirring. Cape reads bigger/dramatic than the
  brief's "starting to stir" — acceptable, it is the cape-first gag.
- **CJ-PL-04 — PASS.** Thumb on the button, drawn fingers readable, white eyes, face at frame
  edge, orange sodium light. Best hand in the set.
- **CJ-PL-05 — PASS (hero).** Whole lot purple, sky block purple, parking lines + ground wash
  purple, suit base stays white with purple rim only, cape edge glowing purple, remote still
  raised, broken streetlight. Stars are cartoon 5-point shapes — stylized, acceptable.
- **CJ-PL-06 — PASS.** Arm lowered, looking around satisfied, cape settling, purple lot.
  Eyes carry a cartoony side-eye pupil — allowed by pupil canon (pupils sometimes, for
  emphasis). Broken/bent streetlight fits "broken-streetlight purple."
