# Cape Jones — Parking-Lot LED Remote Test Short: Pose List (2D track)

All poses drawn in the show style (classic Hanna-Barbera humanoid, clean medium-weight outlines, flat limited palette, silhouette-first) on VOID WHITE backgrounds, locked palette: suit pure white #FFFFFF, cowl & logo deep violet #3E1473, skin #E5B28C, cape & shadows absolute black #000000, white eyes default (pupil canon per sheet 05). No 3D work; no redrawing of the existing repo turnarounds (refs 01/02). Every pose was read back with own eyes and verified on-model before delivery.

- `pose-01-idle.png` — Arms-down idle, front 3/4, full figure. Arms at sides, feet planted, cape draped and still. The natural idle, the show's default pose. Serves CJ-PL-01/02.
- `pose-02-raise.png` — Arm raise mid-pose, front 3/4, full figure. Right arm rising with the small black remote in hand, left arm down, cape starting to lift at the hem (cape-first: the cape moves a beat before the body). Serves CJ-PL-03.
- `pose-03-press.png` — Remote press: large hand close-up (fingers clearly drawn, wrapped around the small black remote, thumb on the button) + matching 3/4 full figure with arm raised holding the remote. White eyes, stillness. Serves CJ-PL-04.
- `pose-04-shift.png` — Lighting-shift hero pose, front view, full figure. Same raised-arm stance as pose-02, whole figure in the purple shift: thin purple rim light on cape edge and suit outline, suit base stays pure white, cape edge glowing purple, white eyes. Serves CJ-PL-05. Note: the remote in this drawing has a small antenna nub.
- `pose-05-lower.png` — Lower + satisfied look, front 3/4, full figure. Right arm coming down with the remote, head turned slightly as he looks around at the fixed light, small satisfied half-smile, cape settling. White eyes (first pass came back with cartoony pupils — redrawn and fixed). Serves CJ-PL-06.
- `pose-06-nod.png` — Nod, front view, head + shoulders framing (tighter crop). Chin dipped in a small nod, quiet satisfaction, faint smile, white eyes, cape settled. Serves CJ-PL-07.
- `cape-01-billow.png` — Cape as a character: the cape alone, billowing high with big sweeping folds, no wind, no hero wearing it, thin purple rim light on its outer edge. Serves the cape-got-there-first gag. Note: first generation came back as a 5-panel cape study sheet; the single billowing cape was cropped out of it (the full sheet is kept as `cape-01-billow-studies-source.png` — a useful cape reference in its own right).
- `cape-02-settle.png` — Cape as a character: the cape alone, settling after the shift, hanging in calm vertical folds with a purple rim light. Serves the cape-got-there-first gag. Note: the folded top reads slightly face-like (closed-eye fold) — fits the cape-as-a-character gag.
