# CAPE JONES — 2D PURPLE LOT: In-Between List (V1)

**Track: 2D** (drawn show). **Short:** PURPLE LOT — 480 frames @ 24fps = 20s, silent.
**Method:** 7 storyboard keys + 10 new in-between drawings, composited as Tahoma2D 1.6.3
raster levels (one 17-frame level, 1280x720), graded per-segment with the real
`production/pipeline/` chains (sodium-dim f1–205, 48f crossfade f206–253,
purple-streetlight f254–480), hard cut to black f469–480. Every drawing below was
read back own-eyes and verified on-model, on-palette, white eyes default.

**Honest density note:** 17 unique drawings over 480 frames is limited animation
(each drawing held 12–24f), NOT on-twos density throughout (240 drawings would be
full production). V1's motion is the drawn changes + the animated grade shift +
cape/drawing changes per beat. Denser in-betweens are follow-up work for parallel
drawing lanes — not faked as done here.

  (board 04 + ib-04b). The very next shot (f206, board 05) is wide and the hand
  is white-gloved again, with no explanation. Gloves on in every normal/wide shot.
  pose-03-press's bare full-figure hand is NEVER used in a normal shot.
- **PUPIL BEATS (owner 2026-11-22 ~07:38):** white eyes default. f337–427 no-pupil
  side-eye = the smug beat. f428–451 pupil appears and rolls toward the audience;
  head does NOT turn, half-smile stays. f452–468 held eye contact, then hard cut.
- **FACE BAR:** every face drawn with comic-book definition (brow, nose bridge,
  jaw, cheek plane) — no flat/cartoony faces.
- **VOICE GAP:** "ah, that's better" is the owner's recorded voice only. The cut
  is SILENT — no synthesized voice, no temp music, no invented audio.
