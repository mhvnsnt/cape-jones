# Cross-show production library — index of everything held, all shows

Logged 2026-11-02 (owner's order, Part B of the cross-show production-library lane). One record per holding, so any show's production material is recall-able the same way the Gumball scenes are: show, where it lives, format, classification, size, named characters, and how hard it is to extract. Machine-readable version: [`catalog.json`](catalog.json) (same records, full notes + extraction plans).

Swept: every `mhvnsnt` repo and release (the whole cn-gigaleak family included), the TRIPPEDD / God-Molecule / research repos' trees, and the local `~/workspace` holdings. Method notes and honest negatives are in the catalog records themselves.

**Totals: 43 holdings — 16 ready, 14 needs-listing, 13 heavy-unpack.**

Two of the owner's leads came back corrected by the files themselves, and the corrections are stated in the records rather than repeated: the Regular Show archive sums to **≈6.83 GB**, not 18.9 GB (no 18.9 GB asset exists on that repo); and the loose Smiling Friends rig FLAs live on the cartoon-vault release (with local working copies in the wizard-gang lane folder), not in TRIPPEDD animation-study. One lead closed as a negative: God-Molecule-Show-Studio holds no production files at all.

Nothing was copied into this repo's tree from the loose ready files (Smiling Friends FLAs, OK K.O. sheets, Garfield sheets): the sandbox write guard currently blocks local binary staging, and the holdings already live on releases/in-tree where the records point. Giant archives were never going into the tree by design — their records + extraction plans are the deliverable.
