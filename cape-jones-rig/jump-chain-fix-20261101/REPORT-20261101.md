## Fix 1 — SPEED (approval 0/1 separately)

Same 62 frames, same keys, same angles, same flight line. Nothing cut.
Only playback rate changes, verified by ffprobe (nb_frames = 62 each):

- 1x reference (layer fix only): 62f @24fps = 2.58s
- 2x: 62f @48fps = 1.29s
- 3x: 62f @72fps = 0.86s

Research: see JUMP-TIMING-RESEARCH-20261101.md. Industry ranges at
24fps: full simple jump ~48f; action phases anticipation 8-12f +
airborne 16-24f + landing recovery 6-10f = 30-46f; gravity rule 1 foot
in 6 frames up / 6 down. The old chain's single jump (f10-f49) is
40f — in range, but its per-key holds and return to apose make the
loop feel slow. Playback speed-up is Mars's own remedy and preserves
every motion.

GIF honesty: GIFs are previews only. GIF encoding collapses identical

## Fix 2 — ARM LAYER OVER FACE (approval 0/1 separately)

Root cause, measured in the old builder (rig-chains build_chains.py
figure()): arms not in top_arms are drawn BEFORE torso and head, so the
head is painted OVER any arm overlapping it — forearms read as going
behind the head. The builder already has the correct path: top_arms
are drawn AFTER head.

Fix, in build_jump_fix.py only: jump takeoff and jump tuck apex keys
get top=("L","R"). No angle, pose, or key changed. Transitions union
the top sets, so every frame where arms are up gets the over-face
layer, and nothing else does.

Measured gate, old vs new frame-by-frame (diff >30, per frame):
- Changed: exactly frames 15-44 (30 frames) — crouch->takeoff
  transition through apex->land transition, i.e. exactly the arms-up
  window.
- Unchanged: exactly frames 0-14 and 45-61 (32 frames), 0px diff.
  Crouch, land, and apose frames are byte-identical in motion.

Visual consequence, stated plainly and shown in the contact below:
at apex the full forearm was previously hidden behind the head/cowl,
so it looked shorter and ended at the side of the head. With the
layer fixed, the full forearm is visible and it crosses IN FRONT OF
the lower face/chin, hands meeting in the centre — that is literally
what Mars asked for ("hands should be going in front of his face"),
but it is a strong look: the hands cover the mouth/chin at apex.
If Mars finds that too much, the next fix is a re-pose (arms wider),
NOT another layer pass — the layer itself is now correct per his rule.
Exception recorded: only if hands are explicitly BEHIND the head does
behind-head layering stay correct. That is not this jump.
