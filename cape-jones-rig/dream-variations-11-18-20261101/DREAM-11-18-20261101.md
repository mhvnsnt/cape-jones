## Method (same as Dream 3–10, one deliberate change)
- Base: `rig-apose-v7c-clean-transparent.png`, 1100×1500.
- Nearest-base-colour recolour, int32 distances, dist>110 untouched, black outline never reassigned.
- Rim: thin 6px, dilate(alpha,6)−alpha, plate pasted AFTER rim, interior preserved 100%.
- Change vs Dream 3–10: in Dream 3–10 the rim was a copy of the cape colour. Here the rim is its OWN colour in every variation, so cowl/helm, suit, cape, rim, and skin are each coloured independently.
- Eye note, stated plainly: this method has no separate eye colour class (white eye pixels sit in the suit-white class), so eyes follow the suit class. Skin IS varied independently per variation. A truly independent eye colour would need a separate eye mask, which this method does not have — not claimed here.
