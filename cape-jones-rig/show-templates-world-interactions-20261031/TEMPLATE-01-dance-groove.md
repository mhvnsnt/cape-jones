## Interaction pattern
A character walks/strides into a beat, settles into a groove, kicks, plants in a split stance, then twists on the accent. The twist is a separate layer on top of the groove, not a new pose from scratch.

## How Cape Jones does it (room / street)
Room version: he strides from the door to the rug, grooves in front of the TV, kicks toward the chair (never touching it), splits, twists on the beat while the room lights shift (JOJO shift on the twist beat only, return to base on the hold). Street version: same beats on the sidewalk, with walkbob + shadow (see Template 07) and a door-glow accent on the twist.
