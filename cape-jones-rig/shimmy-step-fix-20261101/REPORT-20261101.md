## Pattern, plain + technical
Plain: as he shimmies side to side, pick one foot up while the other stays
down, put the lifted foot down, then pick the other foot up, put it down.
Never both feet up at once. Left side first, then right side, mirror.
Technical: alternating single-support / double-support. Double support =
both feet down (centre, stepped, return). Single support = one foot planted,
weight-bearing, the other in swing/lift (knee bent, foot clear of ground).
This is the Contact-Down-Passing-Up skeleton of a walk cycle (Preston Blair
classic chart; Adobe walk-cycle guide; Animation Mentor passing-pose method),
reduced to a side-step: Dance Central side-step = step to the side into a
new standing leg, then close the trailing foot in, weight transfer, no
overshoot past the standing leg. Gait note: planted foot stays fixed in
world space until it steps; a stepping foot lifts in an arc and lands.
Mars's own words, mapped: "Foot1 down, Foot2 up, put Foot2 down, Foot1 up,
Foot2 down" = exactly the alternating table below. The reverse side mirrors it.

Key table (Foot1 = Leg_Front = our left, Foot2 = Leg_Back = our right):
1 centre: Foot1 DOWN, Foot2 DOWN, dx 0
2 step LEFT lift: Foot1 UP, Foot2 DOWN, dx -15
3 step LEFT plant: Foot1 DOWN (stepped left), Foot2 DOWN, dx -30
4 close LEFT lift: Foot1 DOWN, Foot2 UP, dx -15
5 centre: DOWN, DOWN, dx 0
6 step RIGHT lift: Foot1 DOWN, Foot2 UP, dx +15
7 step RIGHT plant: Foot1 DOWN, Foot2 DOWN (stepped right), dx +30
8 close RIGHT lift: Foot1 UP, Foot2 DOWN, dx +15
9 centre: DOWN, DOWN, dx 0

## How the lift is built (industry methods wired, all RUN)
I1 Contact / Down / Passing / Up keys — keys above, inbetweens interpolated
   with the same rig-chain builder (no crossfade, no whole-picture blend).
I2 Single-support / double-support — never both UP; checked on every key.
I3 Side-step weight transfer — dx -30 / 0 / +30, trailing foot closes in,
   no overshoot past the standing foot.
I4 Foot clearance by knee bend — lift = whole lower leg rotated around the
   knee ball (Foot1 lower 30 -> 90, Foot2 lower -20 -> -70), ankle intact,
   NO foot cut/paste, NO detached foot. Lifted foot is in the air, toe
   pointing down/back, which is correct for a swing foot (it is not planted).
I5 Knee-feet rule, per frame — planted leg keeps its base SHIM angles, so
   planted toe direction matches planted knee direction on every key.
   Lifted foot is exempt from the planted-toe check because it is not
   planted; its knee angle (upper) does not change, only the lower bends.
   No FootIn substitution was needed: no key has both thighs angled inward.
