## Industry standards pulled in for the improvement
- Rotoscope / garbage matte repair — hole-fill (H1), industry standard in Nuke/After Effects rotoscoping: a matte with pinholes is repaired, never redrawn.
- Matte cleanup / speck removal — H2, standard matte choker/cleanup pass, with a size floor so thin lines are never eaten (this is why the approved pipeline's M5 opening was rejected: opening eats thin lines).
- Adaptive anchor threshold — H3, standard keying practice: a threshold that is too high drops real detail, so a lower threshold is tried only where the detail touches the kept matte.
- Spline inbetweening / line interpolation — H4, standard animation inbetweening, applied only to thin lines with a single small gap.
- Edge decontamination / edge extend — H5, standard compositing (After Effects Refine Edge, Nuke EdgeExtend), tried and measured, not applied here because it enlarged the dark area.
- NOT pulled, honestly: MediaPipe/ARKit jaw tracking (no model run, mapping only in earlier work), true 3D jaw (no 3D mouth model), other-Titans/Batman/Superman mouths (0 verified files, unchanged).
