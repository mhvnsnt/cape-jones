### Gates
- **No-crop:** cuts are on the sheet's own divider lines (sheets 1-8) or in
  transparent gaps between figure components (sheet 9, every cut ≥8px from a
  figure bbox). No strip was tight-cropped to a figure bbox; all strips are
  full sheet width, so no figure is trimmed in x.
- **Occlusion:** no compositing, no overlay, no alpha change in any strip.
  Contacts use a checker background only for viewing; the strip PNGs themselves
  are unchanged RGBA crops.
- **Originals unchanged:** all 10 exposure sheets + 09-hero-shot-black re-hashed
  after the build; SHA-256 matches the manifest for 11/11 files.

### Honest caveats (viewed in the after-contacts)
- **Sheet 8 is row strips, not figure strips.** The turnaround column is drawn
  across rows in the original, so each 33px strip contains a slice of a
  turnaround figure, not a whole figure. That is the sheet's own design, not a
  cut introduced here — do not use sheet-8 strips as standalone figures.
- **Sheet 7, strip-05 (frame-16):** a ≤2px sliver of the next figure's head
  touches the bottom edge (head top sits on the divider at y998). No figure
  is truncated; it is a 1-2px touch, flagged here.
- **Sheet 7, strip-08 (frame-28):** the figure's feet touch the sheet's bottom
  border (y1530) in the original itself; the split did not introduce that.
- **Sheet 9, strip-01:** includes the column-header row (FRAME / ACTION /
  SHEET / DIALOGUE / TIMING) above figure 1, because the first band starts
  just below the title block. Sheet-9 strips are labelled run-01..run-10 in
  order: the printed frame numbers on the left margin are not in the plate's
  opaque layer (they show in a viewer over transparent RGB noise), so printed
  numbers were not assigned to strips beyond order.
- **Sheet 10-flex, NOT SPLIT (1/10 sheets owed):** flex figures are ~375px
  tall, spaced ~155px apart (frame ticks at 190,344,497,650,800,954,1108,
  1262,1425,1571), so each figure overlaps the next by ~220px. A horizontal
  band cut at tick midpoints would truncate torsos and capes. A
  figure-extraction pass (not a band split) is still owed for sheet 10.
  It is in the before-contact, marked NOT SPLIT, and has no fake strips.
