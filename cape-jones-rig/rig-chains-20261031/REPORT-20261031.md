## Method, plainly (inbetweening correction applied)

Every frame is a fresh render through the v7c rig builder (same ball-centre
pivot math as `movements-interp-v2-20261031/build_interp_v2.py` and the
Ginyu builder): joint angles interpolated between key configs, easeInOutCubic
slow-in/slow-out, TWO labelled breakdowns per transition (arc bulge +10deg
on the largest-moving arm upper at j=3; follow-through +8deg on that arm's
lower at j=5), arm-only overshoot 5% beyond the last key then settle, then
return to the start key. NO whole-picture blending, NO crossfade anywhere.
AIRBORNE height = translation of a SINGLE rig render (dy), never a blend.
GROUNDED = dy target 0, silhouette bottom anchored to the video ground line
(apose bottom y=652 in the 1100x750 video frame) by hip-drop compensation:
each key's dy is recomputed from its own measured silhouette bottom, so a
bent-leg key (crouch, squat, guard) plants on the line instead of floating
with fixed hips. Builder: `build_chains.py`. Per-frame xsheet CSV per video
(frame, kind, from/to, t, eased, dy_rig, height_video_px, GROUNDED/AIRBORNE).

  flex reads ~28px taller than the approved flex file, same as v2.

  01/02; the stand figure itself is omitted and labelled as such in the
  key names. A two-figure stand version is a separate build, not claimed.
- Inbetween ground dip (honest deviation): KEY frames plant exactly
  (651–652, airborne keys at their exact stated heights +/-1px), but
  inbetween frames dip below the ground line by up to 18px (dialects),
  13–14px (walk, ginyu, jump), 11px or less elsewhere, because leg length
  varies non-linearly as angles interpolate while dy interpolates linearly.
  Arm-only overshoot fixed the overshoot part of this; the remaining dip is
  inbetween interpolation, not key placement. A per-frame hip-compensation
  pass would remove it and is the named next fix if Mars wants it — it was
  not silently applied.
- Walk passing feet: a passing foot legitimately lifts (that IS the step,
  as with the earlier mid-step note); contact keys plant. The walk is a
  cycle, not a forward-travelling walk (no background scroll in this lane).
- Flying/jump leg poses are the rig builder's tucked-leg rendering of the
  stated angles; they read cross-legged at small contact size, as in the
  v2 rig style. Mars's look decides whether the angles need re-keying.
