## What changed — Mars's refinement, standing

- V3's proposal was: cape under a shift = a DARK TINT of the palette
  (plum-black, red-black, teal-black, navy-black), silhouette held.
  That proposal is SUPERSEDED.
- Mars, 2026-10-31 16:35 CDT: a non-canon JoJo shift is a good first
  one in v3, and the blue helm / different colourway is exactly the
  type of thing that happens in a non-canon colour change.
- **Cape: it would PROBABLY change from black to a DIFFERENT COLOUR
  also — a full colour, not pure black, and not just a dark tint.**
- **Suit: it might STAY WHITE, or it might CHANGE to a different
  colour sometimes — both are valid, per beat.**
- **Rule: the creators do it based on how they feel / what would
  look cool.** There is NO rigid "cape always X, suit always Y"
  formula. The palette choice is a creative, cool-first choice per
  beat, recorded in the shot spec (trigger + palette + scope +
  duration + return-to-base), not a formula to apply blindly.
  This matches the v3 research: Araki — "None of my characters have
  a canon color palette"; David Production chooses palettes they
  believe will look best, with input from Araki.

## DREAM variation — built example (blue helm)

Base (canon, measured from the rig): cowl purple (90,39,114),
skin (239,175,131), suit white (255,255,255), cape near-black
(23,21,26), black outline (0,0,0) never reassigned.

Method (same as v3): palette replacement, not a gain.
Nearest-base-colour classification, new = shifted +
(pixel − base), clamped. Black outline never reassigned.
Belt, boots and other non-listed parts left untouched.

### Variant A — DREAM: cape full colour, suit WHITE
- Helm/cowl: turquoise (40,200,210) — v3 DREAM cowl, unchanged.
- Skin: pink-tinted (255,200,180) — v3 DREAM skin.
- Cape: FULL magenta-pink (255,60,180) — full saturation, not

- Suit: WHITE (255,255,255) — stays canon white.
- Suit shadow: grey-blue (178,186,199) — unchanged.
- Read: cleanest of the two shifts; turquoise + magenta + white

- Helm/cowl: turquoise (40,200,210) — same as A.

## Cape colour is a per-beat choice, not a fixed row

The magenta-pink cape in A/B is ONE cool-first choice for DREAM,
not a new fixed cape rule. Other full-colour cape choices that fit
the same creator-feel logic (not built, listed as options for a
future beat): coral-orange (255,110,40) against a turquoise helm,
violet (150,60,220), or scarlet (220,30,50). Which one is used is
decided per beat by what looks cool, recorded in the shot spec
first — trigger, palette, cape colour, suit colour (white or
changed), scope, duration, return-to-base. No trigger = no shift.
