## How it went, plainly
It does NOT fit perfectly straight off the front rig — and the reason is measured, not a guess.
The front v7c rig works because its limbs were CUT FROM BALL CENTRE: upper/lower pieces
are cropped so the joint ball is the anchor. The 3/4 sheet pieces are NOT cut that way:
they are fused, asymmetric sheet pieces. Measured top/bottom alpha centroids (native px):
- upper_arm_L: top 57.8/96, bottom ball 26.0/96 (frac 0.27)
- upper_arm_R: top 37.7/86, bottom ball 44.0/86 (frac 0.51)
- thigh_L: top 134.9/177, bottom ball 22.5/177 (frac 0.13)
- thigh_R: top 127.3/168, bottom ball 22.8/168 (frac 0.14)
- forearm_L top 87.4/133 (0.66), forearm_R top 95.4/139 (0.69)
- shin_L top 121.6/188 (0.65), foot 37.2/188 (0.20); shin_mid top 134.3/197 (0.68), foot 39.2/197 (0.20)
So a strict v7c FK (rotate about centre-top, end = centre-bottom) puts the elbow
~30-50px away from the forearm top — that was the v1 elbow gap, viewed in v1.
Fix, wired in build_rig34_frontstyle.py: keep the front FK structure, but use
BALL-CORRECTED elbows — elbow joint = upper bottom-centre + rotated (ball_frac-0.5)*width,
forearm placed so its TOP ANCHOR (not its centre) lands on that elbow. V2, viewed:
both elbows connect, no gap. Legs are NOT forced into strict FK: thigh/shin pieces
are placed by centre + knee-ball cap overlap (the prior, proven, feet-planted method),
because forcing ball-anchor FK on the diagonal shin pieces moves the feet ~120px
inward/overlapping. That is an honest fit limit, not a silent fudge: torso, head,
neck, cape and arms now follow front-rig FK logic; legs follow front-rig layer/gate
logic with centre placement + caps.

## NECK — same width as before, no 3/4 neck
- Source: sliced/Neck.png (front rig neck, READ only, never written).
- Native alpha bbox (4,1,100,79) = 96px wide x 78px tall.
- v7c front rig renders it at scale 1.05 = 101px wide. This rig uses EXACTLY 101px
  rendered width (cropped alpha, resized to 101 wide, height 82 proportional).
- Placement: centre x700, top 330, bottom 412. Head bottom is 391, torso top is 350,
  so the neck bridges head and torso, drawn AFTER arms, BEFORE head (front order:
  neck, then head). It is NOT redrawn or squashed for 3/4, per Mars.
- Visual note: the front neck piece is dark purple/black, so in the render it reads
  as a purple band under the chin, merging with the torso's black collar — it
  closes the head-to-torso gap, it does not read as a skin neck. That is the
  front piece as drawn, not a new colour.

## Front-rig setup reused (not a new system)
Layer order: Cape_Back > thighs > shins > knee caps > pelvis > torso >
upper arms > forearms > neck > head. Arms-up override: arms drawn AFTER head
(arms-up-over-face pose, jump-fix top_arms rule).
Anchors: shoulders L (580,410) R (820,410); hips L (640,780) R (760,780);
piece scale 1.3 (same as prior 3/4 builds, so fit is comparable).
Cape rule: cape_back only moving cape part; collar is fused into torso, locked.
Wrist rule: wrist = 75% along forearm, hand fused at bottom, no hand cut/paste.
Knee-feet: knees outward, feet/toes outward, same direction, by eye PASS.

2. Lean-forward-34 hands are forward over thighs, NOT hands-on-knees (IK check).
3. Pelvis reads as underwear/belt piece as drawn. Ambiguous scoop-top piece
   still unused. Separate cape collar piece still none (fused into torso).
4. Sit-lotus NOT built from these pieces (chair thighs, not crossed lotus thighs).
