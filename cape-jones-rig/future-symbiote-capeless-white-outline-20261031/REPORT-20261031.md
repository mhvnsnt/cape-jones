## Standing rule recorded exactly (Mars, 2026-10-31 20:52 CDT)
**SYMBIOTE WHITE-OUTLINE RULE** — in the all-black form, anytime his body
crosses in front of itself (arms in front of torso, arm over arm, etc.),
the front part gets a WHITE outline, so the viewer can see that his body
is in front of itself. All black on all black with no outline is unreadable,
so the white outline is mandatory for self-crossing, not optional decoration.

## Method: true piece removal (FLA / sliced rig)
Cape Jones is built out of FLA / sliced pieces. The v7c layer stack is:
Cape_Back_Main (back layer) -> legs -> arm uppers -> Pelvis ->
Cape_Collar -> Torso_Chest -> Belt_Overlay -> forearm lowers -> caps ->
Neck -> Head_Front.
The capeless version rebuilds that stack on a transparent canvas and
omits BOTH cape pieces: Cape_Back_Main.png (490x833 in sliced/) and
Cape_Collar.png (360x185). No rectangle erase is used anywhere in this folder.

Measured, rebuilt apose:
- With cape (rebuilt stack): 374,816 alpha px, bbox (248,254,876,1295).
  Original clean plate for reference: 373,846 px, bbox (248,253,876,1295).
  Difference +970 px (0.26%), 1 px on the top edge — the rebuilt stack uses
  sliced/cut pieces, not the clean-plate pieces, so a small difference is
  expected and is stated, not hidden.
- Capeless (pieces omitted): 247,825 alpha px, bbox unchanged
  (248,254,876,1295) — the bbox does not shrink because the cape sits
  inside the head/hands/feet width; what changes is what is INSIDE it.
- Visible cape pixels removed by piece omission: **126,991 px**
  (with-cape alpha minus capeless alpha), vs 85,752 px for the old
  rectangle erase. The old erase under-counted because it left the cape
  above y600 and only cut the side flaps.
- Body: all figure pixels (18,18,22), alpha from the rebuilt stack.
- Symbol: WHITE (255,255,255) diamond only, zone x540-575 y460-545,
  diamond |x-557|/18 + |y-502|/42 <= 1, 1,524 white px in both apose
  versions (1,519 in the earlier variant; the 5 px difference is the
  rebuilt stack's mask). Cowl purple is deliberately NOT whitened —
  the whole head is black in this form, which is the symbiote read, not
  a white cowl. If Mars's symbol is a different chest shape, that zone
  still needs his correction, as before.

White-outline measurements:
- Front forearm lower pieces: 34,956 alpha px.
- Front-over-behind overlap: 23,839 px (forearm pixels lying on top of
  another body part — that is the self-crossing area).
- White crossing ring: **3,458 px, width 4, colour (255,255,255)**.
  Computed as dilate(front,4) minus front, restricted to ring pixels
  touching the behind body (dilate(behind,2)). It is NOT a full
  silhouette outline: the full ring would be 5,542 px, and the 2,084 px
  difference is the outer edge over empty background, which this rule
  does not require.
- Outlined parts: forearm lower pieces only. Elbow caps are excluded
  from the front mask.
- Symbol in the crossed pose: 1,524 white px (same diamond, not obscured
  by the forearms — the forearms cross below it).
- Crossed pose: 202,620 alpha px, bbox (304,254,832,1295).

Defect, not hidden: one elbow cap lands centre-chest in the crossed pose
and reads as a dark disc on the torso (visible in the detail crop). It is
NOT white-outlined, because caps were excluded from the front mask.
Outlining or removing caps in the all-black form is a follow-up if Mars
wants it. Joint balls at elbows/knees in the apose are part of the
silhouette and read normally against the grey background.
