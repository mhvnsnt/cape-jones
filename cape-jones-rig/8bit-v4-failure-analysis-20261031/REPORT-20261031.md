## 1a. Hand-tooled — what was hand-set, line by line (build_8bit_upgrade.py, build_v4)
All three motion curves are literal lists typed into the builder, not rig
keys, not gait parameters, not measured from a walk:
- `scales = [0.85, 0.95, 1.05, 1.15, 1.25, 1.15, 1.05, 0.95, 0.85]` — linear
  0.10 steps, centre-scaled (scale is applied about the canvas centre, so
  head and feet move together; nothing is planted).
- `shifts = [18, -18, 18, -18, 18, -18, 18, -18, 18]` — one number, flipped
  every frame.
- `angles = [-20, -10, 0, 10, 20, 10, 0, -10, -20]` — V1's turn halved.
- The shift is applied in `render()` as: `sx += leg_shift * persp` for every
  Gaussian with `y0 > 200`. That set is 1,703 / 5,846 Gaussians and it is NOT
  legs alone: it is lower torso + hips + cape + legs + feet, moved as one slab.

Measured per-frame lower-body centroids (projected Gaussian centres, same
math as the builder; split by source x0 sign, i.e. left vs right lower body):
| frame | scale | shift | lower-L cx | lower-R cx | feet max y |
| f0 | 0.85 | +18 | 459.2 | 657.2 | 1244.0 |
| f1 | 0.95 | -18 | 410.0 | 644.5 | 1280.9 |
| f2 | 1.05 | +18 | 437.7 | 700.1 | 1314.3 |
| f3 | 1.15 | -18 | 399.2 | 682.7 | 1389.5 |
| f4 | 1.25 | +18 | 435.2 | 733.9 | 1481.0 |
Step-to-step, left and right move in the SAME direction every time:
f0->f1 both left (-49, -13), f1->f2 both right (+28, +56), f2->f3 both left
(-39, -17), f3->f4 both right (+36, +51). Alternating steps: 0/8.
A real walk has legs in opposite phase (one forward, one back). V4 has
neither leg alternating, neither leg lifting, neither knee bending — the
whole lower slab shears sideways, 9 flips in 9 frames at 150 ms (about
6.7 Hz jitter), not a gait (about 2 steps/s, with contact and passing
positions that dwell for frames, not 1 frame).

Gait keys present in V4: 0/5 — no contact pose, no passing pose, no down/up,
no knee bend, no foot lift or heel-toe roll, no hip rise/fall, no arm
counter-swing (upper-body centroid moves only 541.4 -> 563.5 from turn;
arms stay in apose while the lower slab slides). That is exactly the
hand-tooled failure mode Mars already rejected elsewhere: a pixel shift
standing in for a rig pose, not a rig pose itself.

## 1b. Cropped bad — measured, not claimed
Delivered GIFs are 1100x1500 on grey (128,128,128); non-grey bboxes measured
per frame. Transparent-render bboxes in the V4 measurements file agree
(peak y_max 1484 vs 1485 on grey, 1 px of splat halo).
- V4 peak frame f4: bbox x228-985, y125-1485. Bottom margin = 1500-1-1485
  = 14 px (transparent: 15 px). Top margin 125 px, sides 114+ px. The crop
  is at the BOTTOM: feet + Gaussian halo run to the edge of the frame.
- V4 feet travel (delivered): 1248 -> 1485 -> 1248 = 237 px. V1 feet travel:
  1291 -> 1398 = 107 px. V4's centre-scale growth is 2.2x V1's travel and
  ends 14 px from the edge, so any further growth crops outright.
- The builder itself admits the retreat: "an earlier 1.45 peak touched
  y=1499" — 1.25 was already a pull-back from a frame that DID touch the
  edge, and it still lands within 14 px. No-crop gate (margin >= 40 px,
  edge alpha 0) FAILS on the peak frame even though edge alpha is 0 —
  margin, not just edge touch, is the crop test, and V4 fails it.
- V1, for contrast, never gets closer than 101 px to the bottom
  (max y 1398) and 101 px to the top (min y 246).

## 1c. Why V1 read MORE like walking, and V4's leg move broke it
Mars's V1 reading (2026-10-31 20:19 CDT): V1 "almost looks like it is
walking, taking steps towards the camera" — explicitly a READING of the
depth-bulge rotation, NOT a walk cycle, and V1 used no walk keys.
- V1 mechanism: fixed scale 1.0, turn -40 to +40, depth = synthetic bulge
  (distance transform, max 81.9 px toward camera). Rotation moves each
  Gaussian by z*sin(angle), so legs move DIFFERENTIALLY by their bulge
  depth: lower left-right centroid spread goes 249.9 px (front) -> 193.5 px
  (-40) and 198.4 px (+40) — legs squeeze and spread with the turn, feet
  stay within 107 px vertically, whole figure turns together. The viewer
  projects stepping onto a subtle, coherent parallax shift.
- V4 mechanism: halved turn (less parallax, less of the thing that made
  the reading), plus an explicit uniform sideways slab shift, plus centre
  scale growth. The explicit move is LOUDER than the illusion and it is
  the wrong shape: both legs slide together, hips and cape slide with
  them, feet slide vertically 237 px with no ground, no plant, no lift.
  The eye stops projecting a walk and starts seeing a shear: the legs do
  "that thing" — a sideways slab wobble — and the walk reading dies.
  Making the stepping explicit with a hand-set uniform shift did not
  improve V1's reading, it replaced a coherent turn with an incoherent
  shear. That is why it looked more like walking BEFORE the legs did
  that thing.

## 2. Industry-standard walk / stepping / camera-approach methods — gate, not a replacement
No replacement V4 built (not ordered). The gate below is the wiring:
`check_step_to_camera.py` — exit 0 PASS, exit 1 FAIL. Run on V4: FAIL, 6/6
checks (G1 legs never alternate 0/8; G2 feet spread 237 px > 20 px grounded
limit; G3 peak bottom margin 14 px < 40 px; G4 one uniform +/-18 px shift
on hips+cape+legs alike; G5 gait keys 0/5; G6 shift flips every frame,
150 ms, ~6.7 Hz jitter). V1 is NOT failed by this gate as a walk — it is
labelled NOT-A-WALK, which is correct for V1; the gate fails only a thing
CLAIMED as stepping/walking that has no gait.

RUN (in this folder, measured): per-frame delivered-GIF bbox + margin
measurement; projected-centroid leg-phase measurement (same math as the
builder); feet-travel measurement V1 vs V4; gate script above (run, exit 1).
Methods wired into the gate, with sources:
- Richard Williams, The Animator's Survival Kit — walk cycle: contact,
  down, passing, up keys + breakdowns, legs in opposite phase, knee bend,
  foot heel-toe roll, hip up/down. V4 has none of these.
- Disney 12 principles (Johnston & Thomas) — arcs, overlapping action,
  squash and stretch: V4's lower slab moves linearly, no arc, no overlap
  between upper and lower body (upper static, lower shearing).
- Muybridge gait reference — alternating legs, one foot planted while the
  other passes; V4 plants neither foot at any frame.
- Standing Cape rules (already in force, reused, not invented): feet-planted
  ground rule (grounded feet_y spread <= 20 px; ground gate
  ground-feet-placement-wire100-20261031); knee-feet rule (knees+feet same
  direction); no-crop rule (canvas auto-expands to full alpha, margin
  >= 40 px, delivery crop separate); inbetweening correction (real rig
  poses + keys, not whole-picture blend or pixel shear).
- Camera approach: scale growth must be feet-anchored (grow above the
  ground line, feet_y constant), not centre-scaled — V4's centre scale is
  why feet travel 237 px. The size-transformation power already does
  feet-planted growth (54/54 frames feet_y=1500); V4 did not use it.
MAPPINGS only, not runs, with reasons: MediaPipe Pose / OpenPose gait
keypoints (no model run on this VM — gate uses projected centroids
instead, stated limit: splat Gaussians are not segmented legs, so the
left/right split is by source x, not by a leg segmentation); Blender
rigged walk-cycle keys (no Blender render claimed); Harmony/Animate
walk-cycle pegs (trials blocked, as before).
