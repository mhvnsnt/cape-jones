## Method — whole only
- Every piece is drawn WHOLE, exactly as its source file, no crop, no split,
  no erase, no content edit.
- The ONLY scaling is the widget's own display placement size (DEFS w/h),
  applied uniformly to the whole piece, same as the widget stage itself.
  That is placement, not a content cut/crop/edit. Native sizes are listed
  in the audit so the placement scaling is transparent.
- Placement only otherwise: saved top-left x/y, rotation about widget
  pivot (ox,oy), whole-piece flip only (handR mirrored whole), DEFS-stable
  z order (forearmL before pelvis before kneeL at z=4).
- Forearms are drawn WHOLE with their attached fists. The fists are NOT
  cut off to fake a clean hand.
- Separate open hands are drawn WHOLE as their own pieces at their saved
  positions, exactly as the widget draws all 18 pieces. They are not
  layered to hide a cut — there is no cut in this build to hide.
- Note on original cutting: the sitting-rig-34 pieces themselves were
  originally clean-cut from Mars's uploaded sheet, and the v7c front
  pieces were originally cut from the front rig. That original cutting
  is the source material, not a new edit made in this job. No new
  cutting was done here.
