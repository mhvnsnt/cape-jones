## Codec note (honest)
This ffmpeg build silently drops alpha for VP9 and AV1 in MP4/WebM
(yuva420p falls back to yuv420p, background decodes black), and rejects
qtrle/ProRes in the MP4 muxer. PNG video in MP4 is the codec that
verifies as rgba here, so the MP4 is large (50 MB); the GIF is the
small playable transparent preview (21 MB).
