## Fist removal — method, no lie
The v7c front lower PNGs (Arm_Front_Lower / Arm_Back_Lower) ship NO
separate fist file; the fist is the bottom portion of that one drawing.
Previous builds drew the whole piece, then covered the fist with the open
hand — which is why a sliver peeked out.
In this builder the fist is treated as its own layer: the lower piece is
split at the builder's existing wrist line (75% of the piece height, the
same wrist point every earlier builder used) into forearm-layer +
fist-layer, and the fist layer is simply NOT drawn. The omitted fist
layers are saved in pieces/fist_OMITTED_L.png and _R.png for audit, not
drawn. No pixels were erased from the final figure, no hand was cropped
or split, and no fist is drawn underneath the open hand. The forearm
layers used are saved as pieces/forearm_no_fist_L.png / _R.png.
That is why fists are now separate pieces and easy to just remove: in
this builder, deleting the fist layer is a one-line omission.
