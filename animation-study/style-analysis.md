## Style rules (from the Johnny Bravo style-bible)

1. One exaggeration, committed fully → **the cape**.
2. Standard humanoid construction; comedy from proportion, not strangeness.
3. Silhouette-first; likeness anchors locked (mask / cape volume / gold bands).
4. Clean medium-weight outlines; flat limited palette (white / black / navy / crimson / gold).
5. Character design sheets ship with VOID WHITE versions; full arm reach with frame margin.
