# Side rig wrist fix — bone-parent, bent pose only — 2026-11-01

Mars, 2026-11-01 10:36 CST, was right: the previous auto rigs were still
hand-tooled PIL pastes. Hands must go with the wrist, no wrist cut, no
separate hand placement, and no idle arms-down pose was asked for here.
This task builds ONLY the bent side pose, in Blender, as a bone rig.

## Method — research, not a rule list

- Toon Boom Harmony cut-out rigging (docs.toonboom.com, Harmony 20,
  "How to Rig a Cut-out Character"): "you can make the forearm layer a
  child of the arm layer, and the hand layer a child of the forearm
  layer. This way, if the character moves their forearm, the hand will
  follow, and if they move their arm, the forearm and hand will follow."
  Peg pivots are set on joints; hierarchy is torso -> neck -> head,
  arms to torso, legs to hips. That hierarchy is copied here, bone for peg.
- Blender bone parenting (same principle, rigid pieces, no deformation):
  upper_arm -> forearm -> hand bones, each piece an image plane with
  parent_type BONE and parent_bone set. Rotating a parent bone moves its
  children automatically. No PIL paste-and-place is used anywhere in this
  build; the builder is build_blender_rig.py, run in Blender 4.5.3 LTS.
- Blender quirk found by test, not guessed: a bone-parented object's
  origin is the bone TAIL, not the head. The builder stores each plane's
  transform relative to the bone-tail origin (rotation-only bone matrix,
  not the full matrix — using the full matrix double-translated every
  plane in the first run, and that run was discarded, not shown as a rig).
  A small test armature proved +90 deg Z on a down-pointing bone moves
  its tail to +X, and its child hand bone follows to the same +X line.

## What was NOT hand-tooled

- No PIL composite, no per-piece x/y paste coordinates in the output.
  Plane centres in the builder are bone midpoints or bone-tail anchors
  (cape_long top at chest tail, boot bottom at ground, shin bottom at
  ground), and the pose is a single bone rotation per forearm.
- No wrist cut: the previous idle-pose wrist step/cut is not present
  here, because the hand is never moved independently of its bone.

## Honest limits — not hidden

- Head-to-neck piece shape: at the centre column head and neck are
  continuous, but at the face side the head chin overhangs the native
  neck piece's slanted top, leaving a visible gap in the render between
  chin and neck top. Moving the neck plane up 43 px (410 -> 367 centre)
  did not close the face-side gap, because the neck piece's top edge
  itself is slanted in the native cut, not flat. Closing that gap needs
  a neck piece whose top edge matches the chin, or a neck-bone
  attachment that carries the head, not another centre move — recorded
  here, not faked by moving the head down onto the neck.
- Cape long: mostly behind the torso at shoulder height (z -0.20, back
  layer, as a back cape should be); its full length is visible below
  the pelvis, behind the legs, to y ~1192 in canvas terms. It is not a
  front cape and does not wrap the torso.
- This is a bent pose only. No idle, walk or other pose was built here,
  and no approval or repo landing is claimed: Mars approval and a
  PR-only landing batch are still owed, separately.
