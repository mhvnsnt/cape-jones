## Method (same as side-rig-wrist-fix-20261101)

Bone-parent cut-out rig, Blender 4.5.3 LTS, downloaded free from
download.blender.org (this VM had no Blender binary; the tarball is kept
under work/ in this folder). A piece is an image plane PARENTED TO A BONE
(parent_type BONE, parent_bone); the plane transform is stored relative to
the bone-TAIL origin with a rotation-only bone matrix (the wrist-fix
finding: the full matrix double-translates planes). Rotating a parent bone
moves its children automatically. No PIL placement in any .blend output;
PIL is used only for measurement and contact sheets. Trial renders at 50%
(700x800) / 32 samples are marked as such; hero stills are 100% / 64.
